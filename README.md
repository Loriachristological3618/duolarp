## ✨ How It Works (No Tech Skills Needed)

duolarp uses your MacBook's built-in lid angle sensor to know exactly how your screen is tilted. Here's what happens in plain English:

- **Tracks your lid movement** – The sensor checks the lid position about 10 times every second
- **Predicts where you're going** – Between those readings, it guesses the next position so everything stays smooth
- **Freezes your screen in space** – While the lid moves, duolarp captures your screen and redraws it with perfect perspective, so the image stays put in the real world
- **Adds realistic depth** – The glass effect (blur, shadow, and color edges) is calculated based on how far each part of your screen is from the frozen picture
- **Turns off automatically** – When you stop moving the lid, your picture glides back onto the screen and duolarp goes back to sleep

**The result?** Your MacBook feels like a magic window into another dimension.
